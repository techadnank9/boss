What powers Boss

Each one does a real job in every run.

Keeps the record

Every search, bid, verdict, approval and overrule is saved. The agent scoreboard and the analytics come straight from it.

Remembers you

Your lessons and approved orders become memories. Before each run, Boss briefs the agents with the ones that matter.

Ranks the bids

Scores every offer against what you asked for, so a cheap but wrong product cannot win on price alone.

Measures every decision

Each step of each run becomes an event, so you can see which agent wins, how fast, and how often you overrule it.

Tests Boss before money moves

Drills try to trick Boss with a price jump, the wrong size, a sold-out seller and a vague request.
